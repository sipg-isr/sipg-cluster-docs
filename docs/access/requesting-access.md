# Requesting Cluster Access

1. Ask your SIPg supervisor or designated group contact to confirm whether you
   are eligible to request access.
2. Ask them for the approved private account-request channel and the information
   required for the request. Do not use public GitHub issues or discussions to
   request access.
3. Submit your request only through the private channel provided to you.
4. Wait for explicit approval and account-specific instructions before
   using cluster services.

When asking about access, be ready to briefly describe your research group and
the kind of work you expect to run.
If your supervisor or group contact cannot identify the current request
channel, ask them to confirm the approved process with the cluster
administrators. Never send passwords, private keys, or tokens with an access
request.

You should include the following information in the email message:

```

...

<email_body>

...

Supervisor Name:
Supervisor Email Address:

Your Name:
Your Username:
Your Email Address:
Your Mobile Phone:
Your ISR Room:
Your ISR Phone Number:

...

```
